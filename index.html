Recommended operating plan

Use high-touch retention for valuable active customers, automated nudges for developing segments, and selective low-cost reactivation for dormant groups.

1

Protect the revenue core

Prioritize Champions and Loyal Customers with VIP benefits, early access, and personalized replenishment or bundle offers.

2

Recover customers at risk

Trigger win-back journeys for At Risk customers because they represent more than $90K in historical value.

3

Grow new value

Move New Customers and Potential Loyalists into repeat purchase behavior with onboarding, category discovery, and time-bound incentives.

4

Control reactivation cost

Use low-cost email and owned-channel campaigns for Hibernating customers before investing in expensive paid media.
